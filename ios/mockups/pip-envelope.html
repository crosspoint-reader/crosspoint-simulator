Why the two sliders fight

The page presents at an integer scale, so it is 528 pt tall or it is 264 pt tall — there is no shrinking a little. That fixes the sum: the top reserve and the band below the page have pt to share, and they currently want pt. Reserving enough at the top for a small window ( pt) leaves the page ending far lower, and since the top row hangs off the page's bottom edge, the pad follows it down into the bottom corners. The lift slider is what buys it back — but the lift comes out of the panel gap, which is a chassis measurement, so the right-hand column below is the honest price.
