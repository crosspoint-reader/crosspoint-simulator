What the sizes are

iOS gives the window three sizes by pinch, and only the middle one is measurable from your screenshot: 197.8 × 111.3 pt, 47.1% of screen width. The other two are modelled from Apple's own description of the steps — the small window is about two app icons, the large about eight — which puts them at roughly ⅔× and 4⁄3× the measured one. Apple also documents that the largest size parks only at the top or bottom rather than in a corner, so the large-size frames below are the pessimistic reading: they show it cornered anyway.
